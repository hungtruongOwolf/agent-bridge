How this works

  1. My office: all your chats. Each one is a small stall with a helper.
  2. My memory: your notes on shelves. A helper only sees the shelves you tick.
  3. My helpers: build your own helper and put it on the market.
  4. Secret notes never leave. A guard checks every answer.
  5. Coins are demo money. Nothing real is charged.
