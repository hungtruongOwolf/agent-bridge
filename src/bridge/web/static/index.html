Your own helpers

Anyone can build a helper and offer it on the market. A helper is a short list of steps, like: look through the files, think, check the answer, hand it over. You can see and change those steps as a picture, practise on your own files, then publish it. A tester checks it and tries to catch it cheating, and its trust score comes from that.
